GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know before you scan.

What types of QR codes can I make?+

Create codes for websites, plain text, hosted PDF or image links, contact cards and email drafts.

Can I upload a PDF or image directly?+

A QR code stores data, not a whole file. Upload your PDF or image to a file host first, then paste its public share link into Scanzo. Your files are never uploaded by Scanzo.

Can I customise the design?+

Yes. Change the dot style, corner style, foreground colour, background colour and error-correction level. Always test your QR code after styling it.

Is Scanzo free to use?+

Yes. There is no account or subscription required to generate and export a QR code.

Does Scanzo store my content?+

QR generation happens in your browser. The QR library and fonts load from external CDNs, but your content is not sent to a Scanzo account or database.

Which export should I use?+

SVG is ideal for print and design software because it scales cleanly. PNG is convenient for presentations, documents and messaging.
